## Questions by business type
<!-- v2: generic list. If the run's niche (created in `go`) has its own question, the drafter uses that first. -->
Pick one, adapt it to the business, never copy one trade's wording to another.
- Open: "kako Vam novi klijenti najčešće stižu?" (ordinacije: "pacijenti")
- Google (strongest for services people search for): "da li Vas klijenti pronalaze i preko Google-a, kad traže {konkretna usluga}, ili uglavnom stižu preko preporuke?"
- Messages and terms: "da li Vam se desi da neko pita za cenu ili termin u porukama, a da se razgovor izgubi?"
- After hours (from the validation script): "šta se desi kada neko pozove ili piše posle radnog vremena?"
- Already has a site: "da li Vam sajt trenutno donosi nove klijente ili uglavnom stižu preko preporuke?"
- Accommodation: "da li Vam gosti rezervišu i direktno ili uglavnom preko Bookinga i Airbnb-a?"
- Specific angle from the bio or your SM Audit note: a concrete service, coming to the address, work with companies.
