## Questions by business type
Pick one, adapt it to the business, never copy the detailing template for a different trade.
- Open: "kako Vam novi klijenti najčešće stižu?"
- Google (strongest for services people search for): "da li Vas klijenti pronalaze i preko Google-a, kad traže {konkretna usluga}, ili uglavnom stižu preko Instagrama i preporuke?"
- Price and term: "da li Vam se desi da neko pita za cenu ili termin, a da se razgovor u porukama izgubi?"
- Already has a site: "da li Vam sajt trenutno donosi nove klijente ili uglavnom stižu preko Instagrama i preporuke?"
- Specific angle from the bio: photo-based estimates, coming to the address, work with companies, a school and its students.
- Shop: online ordering. Self-service wash: prices, locations, packages, companies. School: students.
